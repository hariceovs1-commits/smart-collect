## Slide 2: The Problem with Traditional Debt Collection

*   **Inefficient & Manual:** Agents spend too much time on low-value tasks and manual prioritization.
*   **Poor Prioritization:** High-value or high-urgency cases often get lost in the shuffle, leading to lost revenue.
*   **Inconsistent Communication:** Agents use guesswork to decide how and when to contact debtors, leading to low engagement rates.
*   **Lack of Insight:** Management has limited visibility into agent performance and what strategies are actually working.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution: Smart Collect

An intelligent, all-in-one platform that automates and optimizes the debt collection process using the power of Generative AI.

**Our Mission:** To make debt collection smarter, faster, and more effective.

---

## Slide 4: Key Features - A Dual-Portal System

Smart Collect provides two distinct, role-based experiences:

1.  **Admin Portal:** For managers to oversee operations, manage agents, and get a high-level view of the entire collection process.
2.  **DCA Portal:** A focused dashboard for Debt Collection Agents (DCAs) to manage their assigned cases efficiently.

---

## Slide 5: Feature 1: The Admin Dashboard

*Centralized Command & Control*

*   **At-a-Glance Overview:** Key metrics like Total Due Amount, Overdue Cases, and Active Agents are front and center.
*   **Case Management:** Add new debt cases, assign them to agents, and track their status from "Pending" to "Paid".
*   **DCA Management:** Add new agents and monitor their performance.
*   **Team Timetable:** Schedule and view tasks for all agents to ensure organized follow-ups.

---

## Slide 6: Feature 2: The DCA Dashboard

*Empowering the Agent*

*   **My Cases:** A clear, prioritized list of all active cases assigned to the agent.
*   **My Timetable:** A personal schedule of tasks and appointments.
*   **Feedback Loop:** A simple interface to submit feedback, notes, and the communication method used for each case, which is sent directly back to the admin.

---

## Slide 7: The "Magic" - Our AI Engine

Smart Collect integrates powerful AI features directly into the workflow:

1.  **AI Case Prioritization:**
    *   Analyzes factors like due amount, overdue days, and past payment history.
    *   Assigns a "Priority Score" (0-100) with a clear explanation, helping admins focus efforts where it matters most.

2.  **AI Communication Suggestion:**
    *   Analyzes past communication logs for a case.
    *   Recommends the optimal channel (Calling, Email, or Messaging) most likely to get a response.

3.  **AI Performance Analysis:**
    *   Evaluates an agent's historical performance (recovery rate, timelines).
    *   Provides a summary of their strengths and weaknesses, and recommends the types of cases they are best suited for.

## Slide 8: Live Demo / Workflow

1.  **Admin:** Logs in and sees the main dashboard overview.
2.  **Admin:** Adds a new high-value case that is already overdue.
3.  **Admin:** Clicks "Run AI Analysis" to get a priority score. The AI assigns a high score due to the amount and overdue status.
4.  **Admin:** Assigns the case to an available DCA.
5.  **DCA:** Logs in and sees the new case at the top of their "My Cases" list.
6.  **DCA:** Clicks "Suggest Mode" to get an AI recommendation on how to best contact the debtor.
7.  **DCA:** Contacts the debtor, secures payment, and submits feedback noting the case is resolved.
8.  **Admin:** Sees the feedback in the "Responses" tab and clicks "Mark as Paid".
9.  **System:** The case is now "Solved," removed from active queues, and the DCA's performance stats are automatically updated.
